STROBEALIGN-HRY vs BWA ALN vs BWA-MEM3 -- FULL BENCHMARK DATA
2026-11-15, gpualn project (cuda/). Summary and verdict: cuda/BENCHMARK_ACCURACY.md, section
"strobealign-HRY: tested 2026-11-15, not adopted". This file holds the complete numbers behind it.

VERDICT
  Not adopted for the human aDNA pipeline. On ground truth (1.5 M simulated reads, hs37d5):
  - accuracy 92.744% vs bwa aln 93.334%; HRY below aln in 14 of 15 length bins;
  - at MAPQ>=25 80.075% vs 86.731%; at equal precision 99.9%: 81.769% vs 89.836%;
  - transversion reference bias (MAPQ>=25) +0.01819 vs aln +0.00952 (significant);
  - either hybrid arm is worse than production aln + bwa-mem3 (93.347%, bias +0.00913).
  Speed (real libraries, 32 threads, saved index): 6-10x faster than CPU bwa aln, slower than
  gpualn on the two low-endogenous libraries, faster on the 94%-endogenous one; 21 GB RAM.
  On real libraries HRY reports nearly every exogenous read as mapped (soft-clipped local hits)
  unless filtered on alignment score >= 60.

PAPER
  Tolstoganov I, Sahlin K. Hybrid RY-mers increase aDNA alignment sensitivity.
  bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.11.05.764514, v2 posted 2026-11-10.
  Code: https://github.com/Itolstoganov/strobealign/releases/tag/hry
  Evaluation: https://github.com/Itolstoganov/adna_bench
  The paper measures accuracy (primary overlaps the true interval and alignment score >= 60 under
  strobealign's scoring), deaminated-read accuracy, runtime, memory, and aMeta taxon recovery.
  It does NOT measure reference bias (its simulation has no heterozygous sites), and reports no
  MAPQ-filtered results. Reads are 40 bp only; divergence is 0.5/2/5% synthetic substitutions and
  indels (Mason) written into fruit fly, ZymoBIOMICS and human CHM13 references.

Strategies and exact options
  aln      bwa aln -l 1024 -n 0.01 -o 2 -t T  +  bwa samse         (BWA1; also the paper's options)
  mem3     bwa-mem3 mem -L 15 --supp-rep-hard-cap 5 -t T           (pipeline MEM_OPTS since 2026-10-14)
  hry      strobealign --adna --ry-len 8 -M 500 --ssw -k 17 -s 15  (the paper's HRY, from
           adna_bench simx/config/config.yaml: versions[0] + main_k 17, s = k - 3 + 1)
  hry50    as hry plus -r 50 (short-read profile; the set's estimated length is 94 bp)
           -> byte-identical records to hry (cmp of all non-header lines)
  hrydef   strobealign --adna                                       (release default)
  dk       strobealign -l 1 -u 1 -M 500 -k 17 -s 15                 (the paper's non-aDNA baseline)
  strobe   strobealign                                              (plain defaults, same binary)
  Hybrids: reads < 64 bp to the first mapper, >= 64 bp to the second (hybrid_curve.py for every
  cutoff; merge_hybrid.py builds the SAM for the bias tests).

Simulated set (cuda/tools/benchmark/simulate_adna.py)
  1,500,000 reads, 20-169 bp in 15 equal bins (100,000 per bin), drawn from chr22 of hs37d5
  carrying NA19471 (LWK) biallelic SNVs (1000G phase 3 v5b; 38,599 het sites on chr22), haplotype
  50/50 per read, La Brana mapDamage profile (C>T 5', G>A 3'), flat Q37 with matching error rate,
  seed 7 ("2206285 position draws" -- identical to the 2026-10 set). All reads endogenous.
  Truth in the read name: r<idx>:<chrom>:<1-based leftmost ref pos>:<strand>:<len>:<hap>.

Scoring
  correct   = primary record mapped to the true chromosome with unclipped start == true start
              (POS minus leading S/H; tol 0). --raw-pos = old rule (POS itself).
  mapped%   = mapped / total; precision% = correct / mapped; accuracy% = correct / total.
  bias      = 0.5 - alt/(ref+alt) at heterozygous TRANSVERSION sites (damage cannot fake them),
              reads mapped to chr22 only; control = counting at the true positions.
  bootstrap = paired, het SITE resampled, B = 2000.

Real libraries (cuda/../test_data)
  sub100k     100,000 reads, 30-91 bp, head of AVA1B (soil), 0.5% mapped by bwa aln
  p17_shuf1m  999,552 reads, 30-49 bp, pipeline short branch, 94.4% mapped
  p1_shuf1m   1,000,528 reads, 30-49 bp, pipeline short branch, 0.2% mapped
  Speed runs: -t 32, strictly one at a time, nothing else running, page cache warmed before
  each tool. HRY index built once with -r 50 (--create-index) and loaded with --use-index.

14. CAVEATS
===========
  - Simulated reads are all endogenous: exogenous behaviour is only seen in section 13 (no truth).
  - SNVs only (no indels) in the simulation, so placement truth is exact; the paper's Mason indels
    are not modelled. Divergence here is one person's real SNVs (~0.1%), the human-aDNA case; the
    paper's 0.5-5% synthetic divergence favours HRY increasingly, and its CHM13 (human) 0.5% win
    does not reproduce here (HRY -0.69 points at 40-49 bp). Not resolved without their pipeline.
  - The paper's correctness rule (overlap AND AS>=60, rescored for bwa) differs from ours; the
    overlap part is ruled out as the cause (section 6), the AS part penalises HRY's short reads
    here (section 8) and bwa aln's 5+-mismatch alignments there.
  - strobealign MAPQ and bwa MAPQ are on different scales; section 5 compares at equal precision.
  - gpualn and G-ALN speed rows are from 2026-11-01 (GPU reserved today); the bwa aln rows were
    re-measured and agree within 1-7%.
  - adna_bench's compile rule uses `cargo build` (dev profile, opt-level 1); if the paper's timings
    came from it they understate HRY's speed. Ours is a release build.
